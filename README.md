# Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes

One joke, one line, about office life — written for a room of tired
office workers who have all lived this exact email thread.

> I replied-all asking everyone to stop replying-all, and 38 people replied-all to agree with me.

The attempt to end the thread becomes the thread: the plea is the
offence, and 38 people prove the point by agreeing with it.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt` (default
`/output/entry.txt`). Standard library only, deterministic, no network.
